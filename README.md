# BOOMproject-branch-cycle

BOOMproject is an e-commerce website made to give people an easy way to browse, buy, and sell products. Users can look through products, see information about them, and keep track of their purchases and sales.

Stretch Goals:

- As a potential buyer of a product, I want to be able to purchase a product.
- As a buyer of a product, I want to be able to view a record of purchases I have made.
- As a seller of a product, I want to be able to view a record of sales of my products.
